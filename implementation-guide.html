Collect the fields currently used by the marketing team: social growth, country, age range, sentiment, top comments, and pattern frequency.

Confirm access and usage rights before promising coverage.

Broad public listening, demographics, comments, and streaming totals are not uniformly available through general developer APIs. Record the source and permission level for every field.

03

Data normalization

Keep raw platform payloads for audit, then map them into one stable contract. Every insight should retain IDs, timestamps, provenance, geography, confidence, and whether the value is observed, inferred, or mocked.

04

Signal qualification

Use recurrence, velocity, cross-platform sentiment, and audience concentration to determine which patterns enter the decision queue.

Surface

Pattern recurrence

A pattern repeats at least 75 times inside 24–48 hours and rises at least 2.5× above that campaign’s normal pace.

Validate

Sentiment consistency

Compare sentiment and top comments across at least two permitted sources. Flag sarcasm and language gaps.

Review

Strategist context

A strategist evaluates artist fit and chooses whether to change strategy, target another audience, monitor, or dismiss the signal.

i
The thresholds shown in the mock are starting hypotheses.

Calibrate them per artist and campaign using false positives, reversals, actions taken, and measured outcomes. Sentiment alone should never trigger a recommendation.

async function loadDecisionRoom(artistId, windowHours = 48) {
  const response = await fetch(
    `/api/artists/${artistId}/decision-room?window=${windowHours}`,
    { credentials: 'include' }
  );
  if (!response.ok) throw new Error('Decision room unavailable');
  return response.json();
}
06

Error and partial-data states

Preserve available evidence when a source is delayed and identify the missing platform, field, or time range.

LoadingReserve chart and metric dimensions; state which window is loading.
EmptyNo qualifying signal is a valid result. Suggest the next review time.
PartialShow available sources and lower confidence or coverage explicitly.
Expired accessName the disconnected platform and give an authorized user a reconnect path.
Stale dataKeep the last successful snapshot with its timestamp and disable approval if needed.
Share revokedReturn a clear unavailable page without exposing internal brief details.
